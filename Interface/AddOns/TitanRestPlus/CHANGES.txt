TitanRestPlus 9.0.1.2 - WoW Forever compatibility update

- Adds per-character Well Rested legacy talent rank support (0-5).
- Attempts to auto-detect a talent named "Well Rested" through the legacy talent API.
- Adds /restplus wellrested 0-5 as a manual rank override for WoW Forever builds where
  the custom legacy talent is not exposed through the standard talent API.
- Applies the configured rank to offline rested-XP estimates and time-to-cap estimates.
- Shows the saved Well Rested rank (WR0-WR5) beside each character in the tooltip.
- Preserves the rank when the active character data is refreshed.

Rate assumption carried forward from the existing RestPlus estimator: base 5% of the
next-level XP bar per 8 hours while resting at an inn/capital, or per 32 hours otherwise;
each Well Rested rank adds 4% to that rate (rank 5 = 1.20x). Confirm the exact custom
server rates in-game, as WoW Forever can change server-side behavior.


Follow-up fix: detect Well Rested through WoW Forever C_Traits Legacy tree nodes,
entries and definitions instead of relying on removed GetTalentInfo APIs. Added an auto
command to clear manual overrides and retry detection.
